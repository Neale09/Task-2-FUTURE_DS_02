
# Customer Churn Analysis & Retention Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn patterns and identifies the key factors influencing customer retention. Using customer demographic, account, and service data, the analysis provides actionable insights that help businesses reduce customer attrition, improve customer satisfaction, and increase long-term revenue.

## Business Problem

Customer churn directly impacts business growth and profitability. Acquiring new customers is often more expensive than retaining existing ones. The goal of this project is to identify customers at risk of leaving, understand the drivers of churn, and recommend strategies to improve retention.

## Objectives

* Analyze customer churn patterns.
* Identify key retention and churn drivers.
* Examine customer lifetime and tenure trends.
* Provide data-driven recommendations to reduce churn.
* Support business decision-making through interactive visualizations.

## Key Insights

* Customers on month-to-month contracts exhibit significantly higher churn rates.
* Short-tenure customers are more likely to leave compared to long-term customers.
* Higher monthly charges are associated with increased churn risk.
* Customers using specific payment methods show higher churn behavior.
* Long-term customers contribute more value and demonstrate stronger retention.

## Retention Drivers

* Longer contract durations improve customer retention.
* Higher customer tenure is strongly linked to loyalty.
* Bundled services increase customer engagement and reduce churn.
* Consistent customer support and service quality improve retention rates.

## Customer Lifetime Trends

* Churn is highest during the early stages of the customer lifecycle.
* Retention improves as customer tenure increases.
* Long-term customers contribute a significant portion of recurring revenue.

## Business Recommendations

1. Offer incentives for customers to switch from month-to-month to annual contracts.
2. Implement targeted retention campaigns for high-risk customer segments.
3. Provide personalized discounts and loyalty rewards to valuable customers.
4. Improve onboarding experiences for new customers to reduce early churn.
5. Monitor high monthly charge customers and proactively address service concerns.
